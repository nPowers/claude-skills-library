# Parallel Research Orchestrator

## Description
Coordinate multiple research sub-agents running in parallel to explore different facets of a topic simultaneously. After gathering diverse insights, synthesize their findings into a comprehensive, unified report.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Not Supported (requires sub-agent orchestration capabilities)
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Define the main research topic and identify distinct subtopics or angles.
2. Spawn multiple sub-agents, each assigned to investigate a specific aspect concurrently.
3. Collect and monitor the outputs from all sub-agents.
4. Analyze and integrate the findings into a cohesive summary.
5. Present the synthesized report covering all researched dimensions.

## Example Usage
- "Launch parallel research agents on market trends, competitor analysis, and customer feedback."
- "Combine the findings from all research threads into a single report."
- "Investigate multiple perspectives on climate change impacts simultaneously."

## Note
This skill relies on spawning and managing multiple sub-agents concurrently and is only available in Claude Code due to its orchestration requirements.
