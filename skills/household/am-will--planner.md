# Household Weekly Planner

## Description
Create a family-focused weekly plan that organizes appointments, chores, activities, and meal times. Use this to assign responsibilities, set priorities, and produce a shareable schedule for busy households.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask for the list of household members and any fixed commitments (work hours, school, appointments).
2. Collect tasks and chores, including frequency (daily/weekly/one-off) and estimated time to complete.
3. Ask about priorities and who can perform each task; note any constraints (childcare, commute).
4. Build a weekly calendar that places fixed commitments first and schedules chores and shared tasks around them, balancing load across members.
5. Flag conflicts and suggest alternatives or trade-offs; add reminders and recurrence where appropriate.
6. Produce a concise, shareable schedule and a task checklist with owners and due days.
7. Offer a printable view and options for recurring templates.

## Example Usage
- "Help me plan this week for a family of four: two work schedules, soccer practice Wednesday, piano on Thursday"
- "Create a weekly chores rota that balances tasks evenly between two adults and two teens"
- "Make a shareable schedule with reminders for appointments and school events"

## Note
Does not integrate directly with calendar services or send reminders; use the output to update your calendar or reminder app.
