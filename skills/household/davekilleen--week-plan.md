# Weekly Time-Block Planner

## Description
Design a time-blocked weekly schedule that balances work, family commitments, errands, and self-care. Use this when you need a structured, realistic plan with buffers and priorities for a busy week.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask for the planning week, fixed commitments (meetings, school runs), and preferred work hours.
2. Ask the user to list top goals for the week and estimate time required for each.
3. Identify multi-day or long tasks and break them into time blocks sized to typical focus periods (e.g., 60–90 minutes).
4. Place fixed commitments, then fit priority goal blocks, family activities, meals, and buffers to minimize context switching.
5. Suggest daily focus themes, commute and transition times, and a realistic evening routine.
6. Present the week as time blocks per day, list actionable next steps for each block, and offer a printable version.
7. Offer adjustments for energy levels, early/late preferences, and weekend planning.

## Example Usage
- "Plan my next week: two days in the office, three remote; include time for exercise and grocery shopping"
- "Create time-blocks for completing a presentation, two meetings per day, and family dinner at 6pm"
- "Make a schedule that fits deep work in the mornings and quick tasks in the afternoons"

## Note
This planner produces a proposed schedule — adapt it to real-time changes and sync it to your calendar tool manually.
