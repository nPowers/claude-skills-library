# Grocery List Optimizer

## Description
Helps organize grocery shopping by categorizing items by store section, identifying available sales and coupons, recommending store-brand alternatives, and offering tips to reduce food waste.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Provide your grocery list or items you plan to buy.
2. Request the list to be organized by store layout or sections.
3. Ask for suggestions on sales, coupons, or store-brand substitutions.
4. Get advice on minimizing food waste with your planned purchases.

## Example Usage
- "Organize my grocery list by store sections."
- "Are there any coupons or sales for these items?"
- "Suggest cheaper store-brand alternatives."

## Note
Does not access real-time store data; suggestions are based on general patterns.
