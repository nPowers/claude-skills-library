# Home Maintenance Advisor

## Description
Provides seasonal home maintenance checklists, DIY repair advice, tips for hiring contractors, and guidance on planning home improvement projects to help protect and enhance your property.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask for seasonal maintenance checklists tailored to your home.
2. Request step-by-step DIY repair guidance for common household issues.
3. Seek advice on how to find and hire reliable contractors.
4. Get help planning and budgeting for home improvement projects.

## Example Usage
- "What home maintenance should I do this spring?"
- "How do I fix a leaking faucet myself?"
- "Can you help me plan a kitchen remodel?"

## Note
This skill provides guidance and planning help but does not replace professional inspections or repairs.
