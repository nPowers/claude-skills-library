# Home Maintenance Advisor

## Description
Provides seasonal home maintenance checklists, step-by-step DIY repair advice, tips for hiring contractors, and guidance on planning home improvement projects to help protect and enhance your property.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask for seasonal or specific home maintenance checklists.
2. Request DIY repair guidance for common household issues.
3. Seek advice on hiring reliable contractors.
4. Get help planning and prioritizing home improvement projects.

## Example Usage
- "What home maintenance should I do this spring?"
- "How can I fix a leaking faucet myself?"
- "What should I look for when hiring a contractor?"

## Note
This skill provides general advice and checklists but does not replace professional inspections or repairs.
