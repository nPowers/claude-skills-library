# Family Health Snapshot

## Description
Collects concise health, medication, and preventive-care information for household members and generates a clear, prioritized health summary and next-step recommendations. Use it to prepare for doctor visits, vaccination planning, or routine wellness checks.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask how many family members to include and gather for each: name (or initial), age, sex, major chronic conditions, current medications, allergies, recent symptoms, and relevant lifestyle details (smoking, activity, sleep) and last preventive care dates (vaccinations, screenings, checkups).
2. Validate ambiguous entries with a brief confirmation (e.g., clarify medication dosages or dates if missing).
3. For each person, identify immediate concerns (new/worsening symptoms, medication interactions, missed important vaccines) and flag items that warrant urgent medical attention.
4. Provide preventive-care suggestions by age and risk factors (vaccines due, screening recommendations, routine checks) with suggested timeframes (e.g., within 1 month, 3–6 months).
5. Create a short, shareable summary per person suitable for bringing to a doctor (one-paragraph clinical snapshot plus bullet list of questions to ask the provider).
6. Offer follow-up actions: scheduling reminders, preparing medical records, or consulting a specialist, and ask whether the user wants a printable or copy-paste friendly format.

## Example Usage
- "Create a health snapshot for my family of four and tell me what vaccines are due"
- "Prepare a doctor-ready summary for my 68-year-old parent with diabetes and high blood pressure"
- "Check my teen's symptoms list and say if they need urgent care"

## Note
This tool provides informational summaries and general preventive-care guidance; it does not replace professional medical diagnosis. For urgent or severe symptoms, advise the user to seek immediate medical care.
