# Household Project Portfolio Manager

## Description
Helps organize and prioritize multiple household projects (home renovations, vehicle maintenance, family events, school activities) into a unified portfolio with timelines, budgets, and next actions. Use it to balance limited time and money across concurrent tasks.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user to list active and planned projects with brief descriptions, estimated budgets, deadlines, and required participants or contractors.
2. Capture constraints and dependencies (shared resources, sequencing requirements, fixed dates) and any hard priorities specified by the user.
3. Score or prioritize projects using user-defined criteria (urgency, ROI, safety, cost) and present a ranked portfolio view with rationale.
4. For each top-priority project, produce a concise plan: milestones, immediate next actions, estimated time blocks, and an estimated budget phasing.
5. Identify risks and potential bottlenecks (permits, availability of contractors, material lead times) and suggest mitigation steps.
6. Recommend an ongoing cadence for portfolio reviews (weekly check-ins, monthly status) and templates for quick status updates (RAG status, percent complete, blockers).
7. Offer simple organizational outputs the user can copy: a Kanban-style task list, a minimalist Gantt outline, or a shareable status summary for family members.
8. Ask whether the user wants assignments turned into calendar events or reminders (provide phrasing and timing the user can paste into their calendar app).

## Example Usage
- "Organize my home improvement projects and recommend which to start first"
- "Help me prioritize 6 upcoming family projects on a $10k budget"
- "Create a one-page status report for our renovation portfolio"

## Note
Does not integrate automatically with external project management or calendar systems — outputs are copyable templates and recommendations. Results are planning guidance, not professional project management services.
