# Personal Budget Creator

## Description
Generate a clear monthly budget tailored to a household or individual. Use this skill to convert income and expenses into categories, set savings targets, and produce an actionable plan with recommended allocations.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user for monthly net income and list of recurring expenses (amount and frequency) plus any irregular costs they want included.
2. Help categorize expenses (essentials, housing, food, transport, subscriptions, debt, discretionary) and confirm or adjust categories with the user.
3. Propose target allocation percentages (e.g., necessities, savings, debt repayment, goals) based on the user’s priorities and provide a default guideline if they don’t specify.
4. Produce a month-by-month budget showing category totals, leftover cash, and suggested transfers to savings or debt accounts.
5. Suggest one- and three-month actions to reach savings targets or reduce discretionary spending, and give quick tips to reduce common expenses.
6. Offer a simple template or checklist the user can reuse to track spending and adjust allocations each month.

## Example Usage
- "Help me build a monthly budget for a household with $6,000 net income"
- "I want to save $5,000 this year — show me a monthly plan"
- "Categorize these expenses and tell me how much I should allocate to emergency savings"

## Note
Recommendations are educational and based on user-provided numbers; consult a financial professional for personalized advice.
