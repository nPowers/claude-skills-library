# Household Budget Creator

## Description
Guides you through building a practical monthly household budget from income, recurring expenses, and financial goals. It creates recommended category allocations, a monthly cashflow plan, and simple targets to hit short- and medium-term goals.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Collect a snapshot: ask for net monthly income, list of recurring bills (name, amount, frequency), variable expenses, and outstanding debts.
2. Ask about priorities and financial goals (emergency fund target, debt payoff, saving for a purchase) and preferred budgeting style (e.g., zero-based, 50/30/20).
3. Classify expenses into categories (housing, utilities, transportation, groceries, discretionary, savings, debt) and compute totals and percentages.
4. Recommend a month-by-month plan: suggested allocations per category, minimum payments, and target savings contributions.
5. Present the budget as a clear table and a short action plan (which bills to reduce, where to trim variable spending, and recommended timelines for goals).
6. Offer alternate scenarios (aggressive saving, debt-first, or balanced) and practical next steps to implement the chosen plan.

## Example Usage
- "Help me build a monthly budget: $4,500 net income, $1,200 rent, $300 utilities, $150 phone, $500 groceries, $400 transport, $600 debt payments."
- "Create a 50/30/20 style budget for $6,000 monthly income and recommend which subscriptions to cut."
- "I want to save $10,000 in 12 months—how should I adjust my budget?"

## Note
Recommendations are educational and should be tailored to local taxes and regulations. For precise financial planning, consult a licensed financial advisor.
