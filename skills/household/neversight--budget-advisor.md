# Budget Advisor and Optimizer

## Description
Review an existing budget or spending pattern and provide prioritized recommendations to improve savings, reduce debt, and optimize allocations. Use this skill when you want targeted, practical changes rather than a full budget from scratch.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user to describe their current budget or paste their monthly income and expense breakdown.
2. Identify major cost centers, recurring subscriptions, debts, and savings goals.
3. Evaluate the budget against best-practice ranges (emergency fund level, debt-to-income signals, recommended savings rate) and highlight deviations.
4. Produce prioritized recommendations with estimated impact (amount saved or debt reduction over time) and designate low-effort, medium-effort, and high-effort actions.
5. Suggest specific tactics: negotiating bills, trimming subscriptions, refinancing debt, automating savings, or reallocating discretionary spending.
6. Provide an implementation plan with milestones (30/90/180 days) and quick tracking tips to measure progress.

## Example Usage
- "Review my current budget and tell me three high-impact ways to save $300/month"
- "I have $12,000 in credit card debt — advise a repayment strategy"
- "Evaluate my expenses and recommend subscriptions I should cancel"

## Note
Advice is generalized and depends on the accuracy of user-provided figures. For complex tax or legal questions, consult a licensed advisor.
