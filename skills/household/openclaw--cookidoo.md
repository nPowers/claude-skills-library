# Recipe Search & Summary (Cookidoo-style)

## Description
Searches for recipe ideas and provides concise summaries and ingredient lists modeled on popular recipe platforms. Use it to find recipes by ingredient, cuisine, or dish type and to get a quick shopping list and cooking plan.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask what the user wants to cook (dish name, main ingredient, cuisine, dietary filters, or available time).
2. If the user provides ingredients on hand, prioritize recipes that use those items.
3. Return 3 candidate recipes with a short summary for each: key ingredients, difficulty, prep + cook time, and why it fits the user's request.
4. For the chosen recipe, present a clear ingredients list, step-by-step instructions, and timing tips (what to prep first).
5. Provide common substitutions, make-ahead and storage tips, and one suggested side or garnish.
6. Offer to convert ingredient amounts for different serving sizes and to generate a shopping list grouped by grocery sections.

## Example Usage
- 'Find quick chicken dinners I can make in under 40 minutes.'
- 'Show me vegetarian recipes that use sweet potato and black beans.'
- 'Summarize a lasagna recipe and give me a shopping list for six servings.'

## Note
This skill summarizes and adapts recipe-style content; it does not access or reproduce proprietary recipe texts verbatim or fetch protected content without user-provided details.
