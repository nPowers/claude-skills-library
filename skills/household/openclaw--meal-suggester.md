# Family Meal Suggester

## Description
Suggests quick, family-friendly meals based on what you have, dietary needs, time and budget. Use it when you need dinner ideas, weekly menus, or substitutions for missing ingredients.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user for constraints: number of servings, dietary restrictions/allergies, dislikes, time available, and budget.
2. Ask what ingredients are on hand (list pantry, fridge, proteins, staples) and whether leftovers should be used.
3. Propose 3 meal options (one quick, one balanced family meal, one creative use of leftovers) and give a short rationale for each.
4. For the selected option, provide a concise recipe: ingredients list, step-by-step cooking directions, and estimated total time.
5. Offer substitutions for any missing ingredients and options to scale servings up or down.
6. Generate a simple shopping list for chosen meals grouped by store section.
7. Ask if the user wants a 3–7 day meal plan or calorie/nutrition estimates, and provide those if requested.

## Example Usage
- 'What can I make for dinner with chicken, rice, and broccoli?'
- 'Give me three family-friendly weeknight meals for four people, 30 minutes each.'
- 'I have ground beef and canned tomatoes — suggest something that uses leftovers.'

## Note
Does not execute recipes or access external recipe databases; suggestions are based on known cooking patterns and user input.
