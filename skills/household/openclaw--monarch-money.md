# Monarch Money Export Analyzer

## Description
A helper that interprets Monarch Money exports and turns them into actionable insights. Use it to generate spending breakdowns, net worth trends, and budgeting recommendations from exported transactions or account summaries.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user to provide a Monarch Money export (CSV or pasted transactions) or to paste key account summary values (balances, net worth snapshot, recent transactions).
2. Confirm the date range and currency for analysis.
3. Parse transactions into categories (using user-supplied category mappings if available) and calculate totals by category, account, and time period.
4. Produce a summary including monthly spending trends, income vs expenses, changes to net worth, and the top spending drivers.
5. Offer tailored suggestions: budget adjustments, subscription cancellations, debt-paydown prioritization, or ways to improve savings rates.
6. If requested, prepare a compact, copyable report suitable for pasting into Monarch or a spreadsheet.

## Example Usage
- "Analyze this Monarch export for Q2 2026"
- "Show me spending trends from my Monarch CSV"
- "What changed my net worth last month?"

## Note
This skill does not connect to Monarch accounts directly—please supply exports or pasted data. Insights are informational and not a substitute for professional advice.
