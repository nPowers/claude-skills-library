# Tandoor Recipes & Meal Builder

## Description
Suggest authentic and adaptable tandoor-style recipes, generate ingredient lists and step-by-step instructions, and adjust cooking times and marinades for different proteins and ovens. Useful for home cooks looking for practical tandoor techniques.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user what protein or vegetables they want to use, any dietary restrictions, and what equipment they have (tandoor oven, grill, oven, or skillet).
2. Propose 2–4 recipe options with brief descriptions (flavor profile, spice level, estimated prep and cook time).
3. For a chosen recipe, provide a clear ingredient list with quantities, a timed step-by-step method (marinade, skewering, cooking temperature and time), and safety tips for high-heat cooking.
4. Offer substitutions for unavailable ingredients and adjustments for different equipment (e.g., oven broiler vs. authentic tandoor).
5. If requested, produce a consolidated shopping list and suggested accompaniments (naan, chutney, raita) and plating tips.

## Example Usage
- "Show me chicken tikka tandoori recipes for a weeknight."
- "How do I adapt tandoori paneer for a regular oven?"
- "Give me a spice mix and a 30-minute tandoor-style salmon recipe."

## Note
Advice is culinary and practical; for food safety or allergy concerns, verify with official guidance.
