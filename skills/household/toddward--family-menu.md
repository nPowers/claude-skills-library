# Family Menu Planner

## Description
Create balanced, family-friendly meal plans and a consolidated shopping list. Use this skill to generate weekly or multi-day menus that reflect dietary restrictions, time constraints, and ingredient reuse to reduce waste.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask how many days to plan and how many people/age groups will be eating.
2. Ask about dietary restrictions, allergies, preferences, and any must-have or must-avoid ingredients.
3. Ask about time constraints for each meal (e.g., 20-minute dinners, slow-cooker night).
4. Propose a day-by-day menu with meal names, brief recipes or key steps, and portion guidance.
5. Produce a single consolidated shopping list grouped by store section (produce, dairy, pantry, etc.) with quantities scaled to servings.
6. Offer prep tips (batch cooking, make-ahead steps) and simple substitutions for common allergens or missing ingredients.
7. Format the output as a printable plan and a copyable checklist; ask if the user wants adjustments.

## Example Usage
- "Plan a 7-day family dinner menu for 4 with one vegetarian night and one 20-minute meal"
- "Create a 5-day school lunch and dinner plan, include nut-free and dairy-free options"
- "Make a weekend meal plan with prep steps and a grocery list for 6 people"

## Note
Not a substitute for professional dietary or medical advice; always verify ingredient safety for allergies and medical conditions.
