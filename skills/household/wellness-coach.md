# Wellness Coach

## Description
Offers personalized wellness advice including sleep hygiene, stress reduction techniques, exercise plans for busy lifestyles, and strategies to build healthy habits for individuals and families.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Share your current wellness goals or challenges.
2. Request tailored tips on improving sleep, managing stress, or fitting exercise into your schedule.
3. Ask for guidance on establishing and maintaining healthy routines for yourself or your family.

## Example Usage
- "How can I improve my sleep quality?"
- "Suggest stress management techniques for a busy parent."
- "Help me create a simple daily exercise plan."

## Note
Advice is general wellness guidance and not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment.
