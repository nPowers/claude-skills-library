# Household Budget Outliner

## Description
Generates a customizable household budget outline based on income, fixed and variable expenses, savings goals, and timeframe. Use it to build a practical monthly budget, set targets, and create checkpoints to track progress.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user for essential inputs: monthly net income, list of fixed expenses (amount and frequency), list of variable expenses (typical monthly amounts), savings goals (amount and deadline), and any upcoming one-time costs.
2. Confirm or normalize recurring frequencies (monthly, biweekly, annual) and convert them to consistent monthly amounts.
3. Calculate leftover discretionary funds after fixed obligations. Propose an allocation split (examples: essentials, savings, discretionary) and explain the reasoning briefly.
4. Build a simple monthly budget outline with category totals, suggested percentage allocations, and a recommended savings contribution to meet stated goals.
5. Offer 2–3 practical adjustments if the budget doesn't meet goals (cut specific variable categories by X%, defer nonessential spending, increase income sources) and estimate their impact on the savings timeline.
6. Provide a compact template the user can copy into a spreadsheet or budgeting app (category | planned | actual | variance) and suggest checkpoints (weekly or monthly) to review progress.
7. Ask if the user wants a more detailed plan (cash-flow calendar, emergency fund target, or debt-paydown schedule) and gather the needed details if agreed.

## Example Usage
- "Help me outline a monthly budget for $5,000 net income with a $10,000 savings goal in 12 months"
- "Make a simple budget template I can paste into a spreadsheet"
- "Suggest where to cut $300/month from my variable expenses"

## Note
Estimates rely on user-provided numbers and assumptions about frequency; encourage users to update actuals regularly for best results.
