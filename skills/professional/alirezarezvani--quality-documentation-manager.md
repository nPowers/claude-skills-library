# Quality Documentation Manager

## Description
Generates, reviews, and maintains quality management documents such as SOPs, work instructions, audit reports, CAPA records, and controlled document templates. Use this skill to produce compliant document drafts, version histories, review checklists, and rollout/training plans tailored to your industry and internal processes.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Start by asking what type of document is needed (SOP, work instruction, policy, CAPA report, change control, audit checklist) and the target audience or department.
2. Ask which regulatory or standards frameworks apply (e.g., ISO, FDA QSR, industry‑specific rules) and whether there are existing templates or a document control system to integrate with.
3. Create a clear document structure: title, purpose, scope, responsibilities, definitions, procedures/steps, records, references, and revision history. Provide a complete draft tailored to the stated scope.
4. Produce an internal review checklist for completeness, regulatory alignment, traceability of changes, version control, and approval signatories.
5. If requested, generate a change control / revision plan: reason for change, impact assessment, effective date, training requirements, and stakeholder notifications.
6. Provide a training and rollout matrix mapping employees to required documents, training formats, acceptance criteria, and tracking suggestions.
7. Offer suggestions for implementing or migrating documents into a document management system (naming conventions, metadata, access control) and sample metadata fields.
8. When asked, produce sample CAPA templates, audit findings templates, and typical corrective/preventive action timelines and verification steps.
9. Conclude with a brief checklist of next actions and common pitfalls to avoid when finalizing the document.

## Example Usage
- "Draft an SOP for instrument calibration in a manufacturing line following ISO best practices."
- "Create a CAPA report template with fields for root cause analysis and verification steps."
- "Help me prepare a document control migration checklist for our QMS."

## Note
This skill assists with drafting and process guidance but does not replace regulatory or legal review. Validate final documents with your compliance team and adapt to local regulations and company policies.
