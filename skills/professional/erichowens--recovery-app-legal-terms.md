# Recovery App — User Terms & Privacy Drafting

## Description
A targeted drafting assistant for recovery-support applications that helps create user-facing legal terms: terms of service, privacy policy, consent language, liability disclaimers, and emergency-response clauses. Use it to produce draft text tailored to the app's features and data practices.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Collect essential app details: functionality, user population, data types collected (including health-related data), third-party integrations, authentication, and jurisdictions of operation.
2. Determine applicable regulatory considerations (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR, local mental-health rules) and flag potential compliance obligations.
3. Draft concise, user-friendly sections: scope of service, data collection & use, consent and disclosure, user rights, data retention, emergency protocols, disclaimers and limits of liability, and third-party sharing.
4. Provide recommended notice placement and opt-in/opt-out flows for sensitive data, plus sample consent checkboxes and cookie/analytics language.
5. Offer a short implementation checklist (privacy-by-design items, logging, security controls) and recommend specific legal review points.
6. Close with a reminder to obtain formal review from a privacy or health-law specialist before publication.

## Example Usage
- "Draft a privacy policy for a peer-support recovery app that collects mood logs and emergency contacts."
- "Provide sample consent language for sharing data with therapists and crisis hotlines."
- "List HIPAA-related risks for a recovery app with optional provider messaging features."

## Note
This skill creates draft language for planning purposes only. Health-data and regulated-privacy compliance requires review by qualified counsel and, when applicable, a compliance officer.
