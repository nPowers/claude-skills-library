# Insurance Claim Analyst

## Description
Evaluate insurance claims to determine coverage applicability, detect potential fraud indicators, and compose claim response letters.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Review the details of the insurance claim submitted.
2. Assess whether the claim is covered under the relevant policy terms.
3. Identify any signs or patterns that may indicate fraudulent activity.
4. Draft a clear and professional response letter addressing claim approval or denial.
5. Summarize findings and recommendations for further action if needed.

## Example Usage
- "Analyze this auto insurance claim for coverage and fraud risk."
- "Prepare a denial letter for a claim lacking sufficient documentation."
- "Review a health insurance claim and highlight any suspicious elements."

## Note
This skill assists with claim analysis but does not replace formal investigations or legal processes.
