# Treatment Plan Generator

## Description
Generates structured, evidence-aware treatment plan drafts for clinical problems: diagnoses, medication regimens, monitoring schedules, and patient education materials. Use it to create templates, compare options, or prepare documentation for multidisciplinary review.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Request essential patient details (age, sex, weight when relevant), diagnosis or working diagnosis, comorbidities, allergies, current medications, and clinical goals.
2. Ask about constraints (pregnancy, renal/hepatic impairment, insurance/coverage limits, local formularies) and the desired level of detail (brief plan vs full protocol).
3. Propose one or more treatment strategies with rationale, dosing examples (with adult/weight-based distinctions when applicable), monitoring parameters, and timelines for reassessment.
4. Include safety checks: contraindications, important drug interactions, monitoring frequency, labs, and when to stop or escalate therapy.
5. Provide a concise patient-facing summary and documentation-ready text for the medical record, plus recommended follow-up intervals and referral suggestions.
6. Recommend confirmation with a treating clinician and specialist input for complex or high-risk conditions.

## Example Usage
- "Create an outpatient management plan for newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes with metformin initiation."
- "Draft a chemo supportive care plan including antiemetic prophylaxis and monitoring labs."
- "Outline a rehab and return-to-activity plan after ACL reconstruction for an athlete."

## Note
This generator is an aid for planning and documentation. It does not replace clinical judgment or specialist consultation. Always tailor and verify plans according to up-to-date guidelines and local practice.
