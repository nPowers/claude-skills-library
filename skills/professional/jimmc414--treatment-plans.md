# Clinical Treatment Plan Generator

## Description
Generates structured, evidence-aware treatment plans tailored to a diagnosis, patient goals, comorbidities, and constraints. Use to draft therapy options, monitoring plans, safety checks, and patient instructions that clinicians can adapt and validate.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Request the clinical problem/diagnosis, patient age and relevant comorbidities, current medications/allergies, prior treatments, care goals (curative, palliative, symptom control), and any resource or access constraints.
2. Identify immediate priorities (stabilization, urgent interventions) and outline short-term and long-term treatment objectives.
3. Propose evidence-based treatment options with dosing ranges, sequencing, and rationale; include non-pharmacologic interventions and lifestyle recommendations where relevant.
4. Provide a monitoring plan (labs, imaging, clinical signs), safety checks (adverse effects, drug interactions), and criteria for treatment escalation or de-escalation.
5. Draft patient-facing instructions and shared decision-making talking points to support informed consent and adherence.
6. Offer alternative plans for common contraindications and a brief list of key references or guidelines that support the recommendations.
7. Conclude by reminding the user to review the plan with the treating clinician and offering to generate an editable plan template or checklist.

## Example Usage
- "Help me draft a treatment plan for newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes with CKD stage 3."
- "Create an outpatient opioid-sparing pain management plan for acute back pain."
- "Generate monitoring and follow-up instructions for a patient starting methotrexate."

## Note
This tool provides decision support and educational suggestions, not definitive medical advice. Treatment plans should be reviewed and authorized by the treating clinician; follow local practice guidelines and legal/regulatory requirements.
