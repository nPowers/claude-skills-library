# ISO 13485 Certification Guide (Medical Devices)

## Description
Stepwise guidance to prepare a medical device organization for ISO 13485 compliance and certification. Use this to plan gap analyses, build or refine a QMS, and prepare documentation and audit-ready artifacts.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user for organization context: device types, company size, target markets, existing QMS maturity, and timeline for certification.
2. Recommend an initial gap analysis scope mapping existing processes to ISO 13485 clauses and identify immediate nonconformities.
3. Provide a prioritized project plan: QMS documentation (quality manual, SOPs), risk management aligned to ISO 14971, design controls, supplier management, production and service controls, validation and verification activities, labeling and traceability, and post-market surveillance.
4. List required records, sample templates (SOP index, CAPA, change control, design history file checklist), and suggested internal audit and management review schedules.
5. Offer a mock internal-audit checklist mapped to ISO 13485 clauses and typical evidence items auditors request.
6. Advise on selecting notified bodies/registrars, preparing for the certification audit, typical timelines and resource estimates, and common pitfalls to avoid.
7. If asked, produce a sample timeline, resource plan, or clause-to-process mapping in tabular or checklist format.

## Example Usage
- "Help me prepare a gap analysis for ISO 13485 for a Class II medical device startup."
- "Provide a checklist for supplier control and incoming inspection aligned to ISO 13485."
- "Create a 6-month plan to get our QMS audit-ready for certification."

## Note
This guide provides general implementation advice and sample templates. Certification requirements vary by jurisdiction and notified body; engage a qualified auditor or regulatory consultant for binding decisions.
