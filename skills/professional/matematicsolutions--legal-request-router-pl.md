# Legal Request Router (Polish)

## Description
Classifies incoming legal requests and generates an appropriate routing recommendation and message in Polish. Use it to triage requests (contracts, IP, privacy, litigation, compliance) and prepare templates for the right internal team.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Ask the user to paste the incoming request or summarize the request including sender, deadline, attachments, and any stated urgency.
2. Detect the request category (e.g., contract negotiation, IP takedown, data subject access request, litigation hold, compliance inquiry) and the relevant jurisdiction(s).
3. Determine priority level (Urgent/Standard/Low) based on deadlines, legal exposure, or regulatory time limits.
4. Recommend the most appropriate recipient team or role (legal contracts, IP, privacy, compliance, external counsel) and list the minimum evidence or documents needed to proceed.
5. Generate a professional routing message in Polish addressed to the recommended recipient, including a short summary, priority, attachments, and suggested next steps.
6. Provide an alternative external response template in Polish to acknowledge receipt and request additional information if needed.
7. Offer a short checklist for the receiving team (key legal questions, immediate containment actions, points for escalation).
8. Confirm whether the user wants bilingual (Polish + English) versions of the routing message or a redacted version for wider distribution.

## Example Usage
- "Triage this legal email and route it to the right team in Polish"
- "Create a Polish acknowledgment template for a GDPR data subject request"
- "Which team should handle this vendor contract review?"

## Note
This tool provides classification and routing support and drafts messages in Polish, but it does not provide legal advice. For substantive legal responses, coordinate with qualified counsel.
