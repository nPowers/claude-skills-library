# Tax Advisor

## Description
Offer tax planning advice for individuals and businesses, covering deductions, credits, estimated tax payments, and international tax treaty considerations.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Collect relevant financial and personal information from the user.
2. Identify applicable tax deductions and credits based on the user’s situation.
3. Advise on estimated tax payments and filing deadlines.
4. Provide guidance on international tax treaties if cross-border income or assets are involved.
5. Summarize tax planning strategies to optimize tax liability.

## Example Usage
- "Suggest tax deductions for a freelance graphic designer."
- "Explain estimated tax payment requirements for a small business owner."
- "Advise on tax treaty benefits for a US citizen working abroad."

## Note
This skill provides general tax guidance and should not substitute personalized advice from a certified tax professional.
