# Tax Planning Advisor

## Description
Offer tax planning advice for individuals and businesses, including guidance on deductions, credits, estimated tax payments, and international tax treaties.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Collect relevant financial and personal information from the user.
2. Identify applicable deductions and tax credits.
3. Provide recommendations for estimated tax payments and filing strategies.
4. Explain implications of international tax treaties if relevant.

## Example Usage
- "Advise on maximizing deductions for a freelance consultant."
- "Explain estimated tax payment requirements for a small business."
- "Provide guidance on tax treaty benefits for a US citizen working abroad."

## Note
This skill provides general tax planning information and does not substitute for personalized advice from a certified tax professional.
