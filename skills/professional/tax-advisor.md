# Tax Planning Advisor

## Description
Offer tax planning guidance for individuals and businesses, including advice on deductions, credits, estimated tax payments, and international tax treaty considerations.

## Platforms
- Claude Desktop: Supported
- Claude Code: Not Supported

## Instructions
1. Collect relevant financial and personal information from the user.
2. Identify applicable tax deductions and credits based on the user's situation.
3. Provide recommendations for estimated tax payments and timing.
4. Explain implications of international tax treaties if relevant.
5. Summarize a tax strategy tailored to the user's profile.

## Example Usage
- "Advise on maximizing deductions for a freelance consultant."
- "Explain estimated tax payment requirements for a small business."
- "Provide guidance on tax treaty benefits for expatriates."

## Note
This skill provides general tax planning advice and should not substitute for professional tax consultation.
